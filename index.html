How it works

1. You set the goal

Describe what you want in plain words.

2. Specialists work

Commander-00 assigns the right assistants. Each one writes its part at the same time.

3. You approve

You review one combined deliverable. Nothing is saved or exported until you say so.

Jarwis writes plans, drafts and checklists. It does not send emails, deploy code or change anything outside this page. Answers come from AI models and can be wrong, so check what matters.
