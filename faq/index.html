Frequently asked questions

Answers about how Jarwis works: assistants, AI models, privacy, offline use and how to create your own assistant.

What is Jarwis?

Jarwis is a multi-agent AI platform. One commander, Commander-00, works with 99 specialist assistants that you can chat with, route tasks to, or extend with your own.

Do the assistants run tools or take actions?

They write plans, drafts and checklists, in chat or together in Mission Control. They do not send emails, deploy code or change anything outside the page. You copy and use the results, and Mission Control saves nothing until you approve it.

Which AI models does Jarwis use?

Jarwis connects to Groq and Google Gemini. It checks which models are available, picks a fast one and a deeper one automatically, and falls back to the other provider if one fails.

Are my chats stored?

Jarwis does not save your messages in a database. Your message is sent to the AI provider to produce a reply. Your recent chat, saved missions and custom assistants stay in your own browser, and you can clear them.

Can I create my own assistant?

Yes. Open the Assistants page, describe a role under Forge your own, and it becomes a working assistant saved in your browser.

Does it work offline?

Pages load offline once the app is installed, but AI replies need an internet connection.

Can Jarwis build my resume?

Yes. The Resume Builder asks for a few details, then writes a one-page, ATS-friendly resume tailored to the job you paste. No tool can promise a fixed ATS score, so it also shows how many of the job keywords your resume covers.

Can Jarwis help me practice interviews?

Yes. Interview Practice writes six questions for the job you choose, then scores each answer you type and shows how to improve it. The score is AI guidance, not a prediction of an employer decision.

Do the assistants use live data?

Some do. 34 of the 99 assistants can fetch live data from free public APIs, such as GitHub profiles, weather, currency rates, tech news, research papers and Wikipedia, and then analyse it. 8 more use job, stock and nutrition sources once the site owner adds free keys. The calls go straight from your browser to those services.
