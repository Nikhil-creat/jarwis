Privacy policy

How Jarwis handles your data: no accounts, no ad tracking, and AI messages sent to providers only to answer you.

Last updated 2026-11-24.

What Jarwis collects

Jarwis has no accounts and no advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs such as IP address and requested page.

AI chats

When you chat with Jarwis or an assistant, your message and the last few messages are sent to an AI provider (Groq or Google Gemini) to produce a reply. Jarwis does not save them in a database. Please do not share passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive information. Each provider handles data under its own policy.

Resume Builder, cover letters and interview practice

The education, skills, projects, experience and job details you enter are sent to the AI provider to write your resume. For the resume, your name, email and phone number are not sent; they are added in your browser. A cover letter request includes your name so the letter can be signed. Interview Practice sends the job title, level, job description and your typed answers. Mission Control sends your goal and the specialist notes.

Studio

When you revise text or draw a diagram, your text is sent to the AI provider. AI art prompts go to Pollinations, a free third-party service, so do not enter private information there. Photo editing happens entirely in your browser and your photos never leave your device.

Live data tools

When you tap Fetch live data, your browser contacts a public service directly (GitHub, Open-Meteo, Frankfurter, Hacker News, Crossref or Wikipedia). That service can see your IP address and what you searched for. The result is then sent to the AI provider so the assistant can analyse it. Three optional tools (job search, US stock quotes and nutrition) run on the server with the site keys, so your search text goes to that service through Jarwis.

On your device

Your recent Jarwis chat, saved mission plans, your resume form draft, assistants you create, your theme choice and cached pages are stored in your browser. Clear your site data to remove them.
